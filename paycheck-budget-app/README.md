# Paycheck Ledger

A single-file, mobile-first budgeting app that splits each paycheck into
"envelopes" (categories like Rent, Groceries, Fun Money) and tracks what's
left to spend in each one until the next payday.

## Use it

Open `index.html` in any phone or desktop browser — no build step, no
server, no account. Everything is saved to the browser's local storage, so
your budget stays on your device between visits. On iPhone or Android, open
it in the browser and use "Add to Home Screen" for a quick launch icon.

The app loads with a sample paycheck and sample envelopes so you can see how
it works; edit the paycheck settings (gear icon) and each envelope to make
it your own.

## Features

- Set your take-home pay and pay frequency (weekly, biweekly, twice a
  month, or monthly) and track the countdown to your next payday.
- Create spending envelopes with an amount assigned per paycheck.
- Log expenses against an envelope and watch its remaining balance and
  progress bar update (envelopes turn amber near their limit and red when
  over).
- Optionally roll an envelope's leftover — or overspend — into the next
  paycheck.
- Tap "I got paid" to close out the current pay period, refill every
  envelope, and archive the period to your history.
- See at a glance how much of your paycheck is still unassigned, so every
  dollar has a job.
